1. Project Overview
Why ABLE?


! The Need
People may need disability awareness, accessibility information, government schemes, educational resources and help — but these resources can be difficult to find in one place.


✓ Main Objective
Create a single digital platform where people can learn about disabilities, find accessible resources, seek assistance, and contribute towards an inclusive community.


• Awareness
Driving community inclusion through clear disability awareness and actionable etiquette guidelines.


• Accessibility
Providing a trusted directory to easily locate verified accessible facilities and public spaces.


• Support
Assistance, emergency/help resources and community support


• Participation
Mobilizing local volunteers to actively fulfill on-the-ground accessibility requests and audit public spaces.

1. Problem Statement
Why ABLE is needed
ABLE – Awareness & Assistance Portal   •   2/12
The Gap
Essential disability support is available, but it is scattered across schemes, offices, websites, NGOs and local networks. ABLE brings it together in one trusted place.


Information Fragmentation
Government schemes, accessibility data and rehabilitation resources are difficult to locate quickly.


Physical Barriers
PwDs may need verified ramps, entrances, parking and other accessible infrastructure details.


Assistance Gap
On-ground help exists in communities, but there is no simple digital dispatch and volunteer flow.

Need: a community-focused digital platform that connects awareness, verified accessibility information, support resources and participation.

Centralized community ecosystem

6. Key Website Features
What users can do
ABLE – Awareness & Assistance Portal   •   7/12


Home Page & Navigation
Home | About | Awareness | Accessibility | Resources | Assistance | Volunteer | Report, with a prominent “Get Assistance” action.


Quick Access Cards
Four high-priority actions: Accessibility, Resources, Get Assistance and Become a Volunteer.

8. Technical Infrastructure & Resources

Open-source Web Technologies
HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript for development, designed to run on free institutional/open-source hosting.


Data & Field Tools
Official circulars, UDID/DEPwD resources and standardized accessibility survey checklists.


Administrative Clearances
College permission letters for authorised surveys in nearby transit and public spaces.

Budget Approach: lightweight web application + free tools + student volunteer network.
