About ABLE

ABLE (Awareness • Accessibility • Inclusion) is a Community Engagement Project initiated by students from the Department of Computer Engineering at Modern College of Engineering, Shivajinagar, Pune.

Our Mission

To promote equal opportunities, accessibility, and inclusion through information, technology, and community participation. We strive to architect and deploy a centralized digital ecosystem that bridges current physical and informational accessibility gaps.

The Gap We Address

Essential disability support is available, but it is scattered across schemes, offices, websites, NGOs, and local networks. Information fragmentation, physical barriers without verified data, and a lack of digital dispatch for volunteer assistance all actively isolate vulnerable individuals.

Our Approach

ABLE brings awareness, accessibility, assistance, and community participation together in one trusted place:

  • Awareness: Driving community inclusion through clear disability awareness.
  • Accessibility: Providing a trusted directory to easily locate verified accessible facilities.
  • Support: Assistance, emergency/help resources, and community dispatch.
  • Participation: Mobilizing local NSS/student volunteers and the public to fulfill accessibility requests.
