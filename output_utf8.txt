1. Problem Statement
Why ABLE is needed
ABLE û Awareness & Assistance Portal   ò   2/12
The Gap
Essential disability support is available, but it is scattered across schemes, offices, websites, NGOs and local networks. ABLE brings it together in one trusted place.


Information Fragmentation
Government schemes, accessibility data and rehabilitation resources are difficult to locate quickly.


Physical Barriers
PwDs may need verified ramps, entrances, parking and other accessible infrastructure details.


Assistance Gap
On-ground help exists in communities, but there is no simple digital dispatch and volunteer flow.

Need: a community-focused digital platform that connects awareness, verified accessibility information, support resources and participation.

2. Proposed Solution
Centralized community ecosystem
ABLE û Awareness & Assistance Portal   ò   3/12
ABLE is designed as a single portal where users can learn, discover resources, request help, report barriers and contribute through volunteering.


Learn
Awareness modules and disability etiquette.


Locate
Accessible facilities and public places.


Request
Assistance flow for community help.


Report
Feedback and barrier reporting.

Core Idea: Technology + verified information + volunteers = inclusive access for daily life.

3. Project Objectives
What the portal aims to achieve
ABLE û Awareness & Assistance Portal   ò   4/12

To architect and deploy a centralized digital ecosystem that bridges physical and informational accessibility gaps.


Bridge accessibility gaps
Connect users with reliable information, support resources and community assistance.


Empower independent access
Help users locate schemes, educational material and verified accessible spaces.


Mobilize local volunteers
Enable NSS/student volunteers and community members to respond to assistance needs.


Promote social inclusion
Educate the public and support inclusive participation through awareness campaigns.
ABLE focuses on practical, local and action-oriented community engagement.

6. Key Website Features
What users can do

Home Page & Navigation
Home | About | Awareness | Accessibility | Resources | Assistance | Volunteer | Report, with a prominent ôGet Assistanceö action.


Quick Access Cards
