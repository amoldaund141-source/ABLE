01 • PROJECT OVERVIEW

Introduction & The Idea

ABLE is an accessibility and inclusion platform designed to help persons with disabilities, the elderly, and injured citizens connect with verified volunteers, report physical barriers, and navigate public spaces with dignity and independence.

The idea is to bring emergency assistance, volunteer dispatch, crowdsourced physical barrier reporting, and verified accessible public transit resources together in one clear, easy-to-navigate experience.

GOAL: MAKE ACCESSIBLE SUPPORT & VOLUNTEERING EASIER TO ACCESS
01 On-Demand Assistance

Quick access to verified student & NSS volunteers for immediate on-ground physical guidance and wheelchair navigation.

02 Barrier Reporting

Geo-tagged reporting of physical infrastructure barriers like broken ramps, blocked tactile tiles, and out-of-order lifts.

03 Directory & Resources

Curated directory of accessible public spots, inclusive transit maps, legal rights awareness, and assistive schemes.
